Frequently asked questions

How do I receive my Discord template?

Join our Discord server and type /redeem with the order number from your confirmation email and the email you used at checkout. Our bot then sends you the template link and guide as a direct message.

The bot does not find my order.

Check that you entered the order number and the exact email from your purchase. If it still doesn't work, open a ticket on Discord with your order number.

What do I need to run the bot code?

A PC, Raspberry Pi or VPS that stays online, plus Node.js or Python as listed on the product. The free guide walks you through every step.

Can I return digital products?

Digital products are delivered immediately. If you agree to this at checkout, your right of withdrawal ends once delivery has started. If something doesn't work as described, contact us and we will find a solution.

I have a question about my merch order.

Merch is printed after you order. Delivery details are on the shipping page. For anything else, email us with your order number.

Can I resell templates or code?

No. Purchases are licensed for your own servers and projects. Reselling or sharing is not permitted.
